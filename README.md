## Open Source E-Procurement Platforms

Self-hosted software and open platforms for managing purchasing requisitions, quotes, vendor catalogs, and purchase orders.

- [Procuman Community Edition](https://github.com/procuman/procuman-community-edition) — Open-source e-procurement application featuring purchase requisitions, supplier catalogs, purchase orders, and e-tendering.
- [ERPNext Procurement Module](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext) — Open-source purchasing system supporting supplier quotes, auto-reordering, purchase requests, and receiving logs.
- [Odoo Purchase Management](https://github.com/odoo/odoo) — Integrated open-source purchase module for RFQs, purchase orders, vendor bill matching, and inventory updates.

## Enterprise Source-to-Pay (S2P) Suites

Cloud platforms providing end-to-end management of corporate spend, direct/indirect sourcing, and accounts payable matching.

- [SAP Ariba](https://www.ariba.com/) — Large-scale global procure-to-pay and sourcing platform connecting buyers to the SAP Business Network.
- [Coupa Spend Management](https://www.coupa.com/) — Enterprise spend management suite covering strategic sourcing, e-procurement, invoicing, and spend analytics.
- [GEP SMART](https://www.gep.com/software/gep-smart) — AI-driven procurement software suite offering unified source-to-pay capabilities and supplier risk tracking.
- [Ivalua](https://www.ivalua.com/) — Enterprise source-to-pay platform specializing in indirect spend management, sourcing, and e-invoicing.

## Procurement Intake & Orchestration

Tools designed to simplify purchasing requests for employees while orchestrating cross-departmental approval workflows.

- [Zip](https://zip.co/) — Procurement orchestration platform designed to centralize purchasing intake, multi-tier approvals, and vendor onboarding.
- [Tonkean Procurement Orchestration](https://www.tonkean.com/) — No-code workflow orchestration engine that streamlines intake and automated approval logic across existing ERP tools.
- [Orocube Procurement Intake](https://github.com/orocube) — Open-source request orchestration framework for routing internal procurement inquiries and budget approvals.

## Supplier & Contract Management

Specialized software modules designed for evaluating vendor risk, tracking performance, and executing procurement contracts.

### Supplier Relationship Management (SRM)

- [Market Dojo Sourcing](https://marketdojo.com/) — On-demand e-sourcing application designed to run RFQs, supplier evaluations, and online reverse auctions.
- [HICX Supplier Experience Platform](https://www.hicx.com/) — Enterprise platform designed to centralize master supplier data, compliance onboarding, and performance tracking.

### Contract Lifecycle Management (CLM)

- [Agiloft CLM](https://www.agiloft.com/) — Configurable contract management suite automating contract creation, approval workflows, and renewal alerts.
- [DocuSign CLM](https://www.docusign.com/) — Enterprise contract engine integrating e-signature pipelines into purchasing agreements and vendor contracts.
